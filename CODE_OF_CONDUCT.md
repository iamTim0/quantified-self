# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Criticise code, not the person who wrote it. Assume the other
person is competent and acting in good faith until you have real evidence
otherwise, and say what you mean plainly rather than sharply.

## What is expected

- Keep discussion technical and specific. "This query is missing a `tenant_id`
  filter" is useful; "this is amateur work" is not.
- Accept that people have different levels of context. A question that seems
  obvious to you may be the first time somebody has met this codebase.
- Disagree about the work without making it about the author. When a decision has
  been made and explained, let it be made.
- Respect the privacy of anyone whose data touches this project. This is a health
  data platform: do not paste real health data, location traces or screenshots
  containing either into an issue, a pull request or a discussion. Fixtures are
  synthetic for a reason.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments; discrimination or personal attacks on
the basis of who somebody is; sexual attention or imagery; publishing private
information without permission; sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and code review
— anywhere the project is the setting.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through their GitHub profile, or via
the **Report a vulnerability** form on the repository's Security tab, which is a
private channel and works for this too.

Reports are handled confidentially. As a single-maintainer project the response is
best effort, not a formal process with deadlines — you will get a straight answer
about what will happen rather than a procedural one.

## Consequences

Depending on what happened: a private word, a public correction, having a comment
removed, or being blocked from the repository. The maintainer decides, and will say
which it is and why.

## Attribution

The spirit and much of the structure follow the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, adapted for a small project and for the fact that this one handles
health data.
